# Desk Doodles — Submission / Promo VIDEO Direction (how ours becomes the best)

*Source: the same video-analysis workflow (`wf_519620d3-90d`, 2026-06-15) that produced CRAFT-VISUAL-PASS.md. The two
references are PROMO FILMS — "Exquisite Corpse" (Config Makeathon) and "Babbu" (kids' dictionary). This doc extracts
their FILM-MAKING craft (structure, pacing, motion, beats) and turns it into a shot-by-shot plan for OUR video. The
app-craft lives in CRAFT-VISUAL-PASS.md; this is the camera/edit/story layer.*

**One rule above all (from both refs): the film is ONE WORLD.** Same palette, same paper, same ink, same hand — title
card → every UI surface → outro. A single foreign font or stock transition breaks the spell. Desk Doodles' world =
**warm paper + ink-black, editorial serif + tracked micro-caps, hand-drawn objects.** Everything in the video lives
inside it.

---

## 1. WHAT THE REFERENCE FILMS DO (the bar)

- **Tight narrative arc, ~40s:** title → who/why (persona) → the product doing its thing (clean demo) → the "wow"
  reveal → brand outro. No dead air; every beat earns its seconds.
- **Two motion timescales at once:** a slow continuous drift/Ken-Burns UNDER fast per-element pops. The frame is never
  static, never frantic.
- **One-element-at-a-time reveal cascade** with a slight scale-overshoot/settle — staggered reads as *crafted*;
  simultaneous reads as a *page load*.
- **The product's core idea IS the hero motion beat.** Exquisite Corpse: masked seams → full assembled-figure reveal.
  Babbu: the baby-word→real-word width-tween IS the thesis, made visible, repeated, emotional. **Our equivalent is the
  2D-ink → 3D-object flip — that has to be the centerpiece shot, not a throwaway.**
- **Type breathes on entry** (organic settle / morphing wordmark), never a hard cut. Bookends the film with the same
  hand-cut energy.
- **Reward beat sized to the achievement** (confetti for the full flow, not a toast).
- **Surgical realism for trust:** a few photoreal touches (live status bar, recording dot, blinking cursor, real
  cursor moving with intent) make a playful flat brand read as a *genuine shipping app*.
- **Scene grammar:** crossfade-through-white between scenes, fade-to-black at the end, bookend open-small → close-
  fuller-and-centered.
- **A point of view:** witty naming, a culture, inside jokes — feels made by people with taste, not generated to spec.

## 2. THE DESK DOODLES VIDEO — proposed arc (~35–45s)

> Shot count is a guide; the BEATS and their order are the point. Record real app footage at high res; composite into
> the paper world.

1. **COLD OPEN — the wordmark breathes (2–3s).** "Desk Doodles" hand-settles onto warm paper over a faint dotted grid;
   one ink doodle scratches itself in. Establish world + hand instantly. *(maps: type-breathes, material-is-material)*
2. **THE PROMISE (3–4s).** The line "Doodle what's on your desk." with the radial bloom; 2–3 hand-drawn desk objects
   drift in (Ken-Burns under, per-object pop over). *(two timescales + reveal cascade)*
3. **DRAW IT (5–7s).** Real capture: a cursor (custom crosshair) sketches a mug / gameboy on the draw panel —
   perfect-freehand ink flowing, a style flip or two (bold-ink → sketchy) to show range. Keep the chrome in-hand. *(one
   hand on chrome; show the medium has personality)*
4. **★ THE HERO BEAT — 2D → 3D (5–8s, the centerpiece).** The flat ink doodle ANTICIPATES (tenses), then **stands up
   off the page** into its 3D form with ease-in → overshoot → settle, then a slow auto-orbit that HOLDS the best ¾
   angle. This is the whole pitch — give it the most seconds, the cleanest framing, a beat of silence/hold. *(core idea
   = hero motion; show don't tell)*
5. **RESTYLE / RANGE (4–5s):** quick cascade of the same object in distinct 3D treatments + a couple other doodles
   flipping — proof of the system, one-at-a-time pops. *(reveal cascade)*
6. **DROP IT ON THE WALL (4–6s):** place the object onto the public desk; other people's doodles already there; a
   **reward beat** fires — ink-black torn-paper confetti + a counter tick (on-palette, sized to the moment). *(reward
   beat, on-system)*
7. **BRAND OUTRO (3–4s):** the wordmark re-forms (morph/settle), tagline + URL, **fade-to-black**. Bookend: opened on
   one doodle, close on the full living desk. *(scene grammar, bookend)*

**Editing grammar:** crossfade-through-warm-white between scenes; one slow push/drift running under the whole thing;
cuts land on motion, never on stillness. Keep a click/scratch foley layer if sound is allowed (the refs imply audio
does narrative work).

## 3. NON-NEGOTIABLES (what makes ours surpass, not just match)

- **The flip is the star.** Their hero beat is a reveal/tween; OURS is a genuine dimensional transform of the user's
  OWN drawing. Shoot it like the money shot — best angle, best lighting (needs the CRAFT-VISUAL-PASS R1–R5 to have
  landed so the 3D looks premium on camera: contact shadow, env, value range, Inflate-lead).
- **Ink-black holds on screen.** No hue, no grey-wash; tonal life comes from light/AO/etch. A grey 3D blob on camera
  would read as a render error — verify the 3D looks right at video resolution BEFORE shooting.
- **One hand, zero font leaks.** Every label on screen in our editorial/hand register.
- **Personality:** named demo doodles, a witty line or two, a point of view — so it feels authored.
- **Realism touches:** a real moving cursor with intent, a live counter, the actual public wall populating — sells it
  as a shipping product.
