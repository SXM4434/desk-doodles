## 1. Three post-copy variants (different hooks — pick ONE voice)

All three are honest, all carry the required tags, all stay ≈1–2 sentences on *what it is + how it was built* (the guidelines ask for exactly that). They differ only in the **hook** — the first line that decides whether the scroll stops. Pick by voice; the body + tags are interchangeable.

> **Length note per platform:** X caps ~280 chars (Variant A and C fit; B needs the trim shown). IG/LinkedIn have no tight cap — use the full body + the extended tail. Keep the **video first**; copy is the read-after.

### Variant A — "the wedge flip" (recommended for X / Community-Favorite energy)
*Hook = the thesis-in-one-gesture; matches the cold-open caption so the clip and the copy rhyme.*

> **You drew this. Now spin it.**
>
> Desk Doodles is a shared public desk where you sketch the little things around you, restyle them with a real hand-drawn engine, and flip them between 2D and 3D — and your hand's wobble survives the round-trip. Most sketch-to-3D tools sanitize your line; this one keeps it.
>
> Built solo for the Config Makeathon. Authored locally and shipped live as a Figma Make site, with motion assets made in Figma Weave.
>
> #ConfigMakeathon @figma

- **Source of hook:** `the-story.md` §1 (the locked cold-open caption + share-bait line).
- **Why recommended:** it's the muted-autoplay share-bait moment and the whole thesis in one line (`prize-positioning.md` §5); the copy hook == the on-screen caption, which reinforces the clip.

### Variant B — "the honest problem" (recommended for LinkedIn / build-in-public audience)
*Hook = the human problem, then the bet. Warmer, slower, reads as a maker's note.*

> **I sketch at my desk constantly. Those drawings die in a notebook.**
>
> So for the Config Makeathon I built Desk Doodles — a shared desk where everyone's doodles live together. Draw something, restyle it with a hand-drawn rendering engine, flip it between 2D and 3D, and leave it next to a stranger's. The bet the whole thing rests on: your hand's character is the material, not noise — so it survives the trip to 3D and back.
>
> Solo build. Authored locally, deployed and run live as a Figma Make site, with the video's motion assets made in Figma Weave. Everything's documented in public.
>
> #ConfigMakeathon @figma

- **X-trim (≤280):** *"I sketch at my desk constantly — those drawings die in a notebook. So I built Desk Doodles: a shared desk where you restyle doodles and flip them 2D↔3D, and your hand survives the round-trip. Solo, live Figma Make site, motion in Weave. #ConfigMakeathon @figma"*
- **Source of hook:** `demo-video-plan.md` §4 shot-2 VO (Sebs's actual narration line) + `the-story.md` §2.
- **Why this one:** "documented in public" nudges the **Build-in-Public** judge who finds the post; LinkedIn rewards the maker-note register.

### Variant C — "the one-desk-for-everybody" (recommended for Instagram / place-not-tool framing)
*Hook = the communal surface. Leans on the social-desk metaphor (the Community-Favorite asset).*

> **It's one desk for everybody.**
>
> Desk Doodles is a live shared desk: draw the little things around you, restyle them with a hand-drawn engine, flip them between 2D and 3D, and leave them next to everyone else's. Your hand's wobble survives the round-trip — sketch in, sketch out, signature intact.
>
> Built solo for the Config Makeathon — authored locally, live as a Figma Make site, motion made in Figma Weave.
>
> #ConfigMakeathon @figma

- **Source of hook:** `the-story.md` §1 quotable spares ("It's one desk for everybody" + "Sketch in, sketch out, signature intact").
