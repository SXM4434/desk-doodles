# ChatGPT → "Voice Profile of Sebastian" prompt

**How to use:** paste the block below into ChatGPT (the account that's helped you before, so it has context). Answer its questions if it asks any. Then paste ChatGPT's resulting **voice profile back to Claude** — Claude will use it to write taglines, the social post, video captions, and microcopy in your actual voice (and to reword anything that drifted generic). Pairs with `docs/submission/sebs-voice-guide.md` (Claude's reverse-engineered first pass).

---

## PASTE THIS INTO CHATGPT

> You've helped me (Sebastian) before. I need you to write a **VOICE PROFILE of me** that another AI assistant (Claude) can use to write copy in my authentic voice — taglines, social posts, captions, microcopy — across my projects. Right now it's for **Desk Doodles**, a makeathon app where you doodle the little things on your desk, restyle them with a real hand-drawn engine, and flip them between hand-drawn 2D and 3D, on a shared public desk.
>
> Draw on everything you know about how I actually write and talk. If you're missing something important, ask me up to **5 sharp questions first**, then produce the profile.
>
> Output the profile in exactly this structure:
> 1. **Voice summary** — how I sound, in 3–4 sentences.
> 2. **Tone descriptors** — 5–8 adjectives, one line each on what they mean for me.
> 3. **Vocabulary** — words/phrasings I reach for; words/registers I avoid.
> 4. **Rhythm & sentence shape** — length, fragments, punctuation habits, how I open and close.
> 5. **Register & attitude** — warmth, the kind of humor I use, how I handle pride without hype, how I take small/ordinary things seriously.
> 6. **Bilingual notes** — when and how Spanish surfaces (if it does), and when it shouldn't.
> 7. **8 example lines** — IN-VOICE vs OFF-VOICE pairs (the same idea said my way vs. a generic-ad way).
> 8. **Hard DON'Ts** — the things that instantly sound not-like-me (e.g., punchy imperative ad-couplets like "You drew this. Now spin it.", hype words, "simplifies/revolutionizes/effortless," try-hard taglines).
>
> Keep it concrete and directly usable by another AI. **Do not invent biographical facts** — if you're unsure, ask me or leave it out. Make it portable so I can reuse it across projects, not just this one.

---

_When you get the profile back, paste it to Claude and say "here's my voice profile" — Claude will save it and retune the submission copy to it._
